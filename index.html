Bio

Souvick Bera

PhD Candidate · Colorado School of Mines

I am currently a doctoral candidate in Applied Mathematics and Statistics department at Colorado School of Mines , where I primariy work on frequency-domain methods for spatial and time series data. I develop statistical tools to understand dependence and quantify uncertainty, with particular attention to irregular sampling, spectral bias, and robustness to periodic contamination. I am being advised by Dr. Soutir Bandyopadhyay and Dr. Haihan Yu.

Research Interests

My research interests include Spatial statistics, Freqeuncy-domain analysis, Resampling, Empirical Likelihood, and Climate uncertainty.

Research Overview

My current research includes developing a hybrid frequency-domain bootstrap for spatial spectral averages, characterizing and reducing spectral bias under general sampling designs, and constructing robust empirical likelihood methods using optimal transport. I also study uncertainty in climate projections, using unbalanced ensemble data to distinguish internal variability, model differences, and variation across selected emissions scenarios.
