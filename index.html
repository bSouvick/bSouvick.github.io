Bio

Souvick Bera

PhD Candidate · Colorado School of Mines

My research focuses on frequency-domain methods for spatial and time series data. I develop statistical tools to understand dependence and quantify uncertainty, with particular attention to irregular sampling, spectral bias, and robustness to periodic contamination. I am being advised by Dr. Soutir Bandyopadhyay and Dr. Haihan Yu. Prior to that, I completed my master's and bachelor's degrees in Statistics at the University of Calcutta and RKMRC Narendrapur, respectively.

Primary Work

My work includes developing a hybrid frequency-domain bootstrap for spatial spectral averages, characterizing and reducing spectral bias under general sampling designs, and constructing robust empirical likelihood methods using optimal transport. I also study uncertainty in climate projections using unbalanced ensemble data.

Research Interests

Spatial statistics, spectral inference, bootstrap and subsampling methods, empirical likelihood, and climate uncertainty.
